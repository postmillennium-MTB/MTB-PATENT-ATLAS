Pivot positions are read off Figures 1 and 2 of the patent, so the numbers are approximate. The wheel link (chainstay) turns about the main pivot, which is why the axle path is an arc. The brake link (seat stay) joins it at the axle and drives the rocker, which compresses the shock. The instant center is the intersection of the wheel link's line and the line through the rocker's two pivots, the definition used in the patent. Scale comes from the tire: a 29 × 2.5 tire is about 740 mm across, which sets one drawing unit to 1.15 mm.
