ABSTRACT

A cycle frame having a generally hollow unitary com
ponent of composite material connecting at least two
support members. The frame component includes at
least one pressure molded juncture comprising overlap

ping layers of resin impregnated fibrous material pro
vided by overlapped parts of at least two adjacent wall

sections.
