ABSTRACT

A wheel for light vehicles and a disc member used
therein. The wheel comprising a hub, a rim and a pair of
disc members detachably coupled with the hub and the
rim, wherein the wheel has the advantage of a spoke

wheel that it is lightweight and has sufficient strength as
well as the advantage of a disc wheel that it has less air
resistance when it rotates, and further the wheel can be

easily repaired when it is damaged.
