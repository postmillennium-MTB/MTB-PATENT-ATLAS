ABSTRACT

A steering bearing assembly includes a cap and a bearing
element that define an axial force transmission path there
through. A compression ring is moveably coupled to the cap
and is disposed radially inwardly of the bearing assembly
outside of the axial force transmission path. The compression

ring includes an inner annular surface adapted to transmit a

radial compressive force from the cap to a steerer element. A
steering assembly, and method for assembling the steering
assembly, also are provided.
