ABSTRACT

An improved bicycle bottom bracket system and methods of
manufacturing and installing Such a system. The improved
system may include bearings mounted within bearing cups on
either side of the bottom bracket shell of a bicycle frame. The
bearing cups may be threaded together, and one bearing cup
may include a collet portion expandable by the other bearing
Cup.
