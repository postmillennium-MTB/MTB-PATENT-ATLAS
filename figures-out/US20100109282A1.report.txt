ABSTRACT

The invention relates to Suspension systems comprising, in
certain embodiments, a linkage arrangement including a
shock link, wheel link, rate link, and first shock pivot, posi
tioned so that a first shock pivot is moved in a downward and
rearward direction as the Suspension is compressed, while

said wheel link and shock link rotate in opposite directions,
and so that a leverage ratio is tactically controlled through
said linkage arrangement.
