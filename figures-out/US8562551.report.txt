ABSTRACT

(2006.01)

A neck brace (10) is disclosed which comprises two sections
(12, 14) which are releasably connected to one another along
a split line (18). The ring has upwardly facing surfaces (30.
76.1, 20.2) which limit tilting movement of a helmeted head
in all directions. The brace further has a column (68, 70)
which extends downwardly from the ring for transferring
loads to the wearer's back on each side of the spine.
