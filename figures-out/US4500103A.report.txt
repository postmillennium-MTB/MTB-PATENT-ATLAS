ABSTRACT

The frame of a racing type bicycle is made very rigid to
increase the power transmission efficiency and to im
prove the handling and riding characteristics. The pre
ferred configuration uses large diameter tubing in alu
minum alloy to achieve a lighter, stronger and much
more rigid frame than prior art.
