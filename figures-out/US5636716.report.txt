A link base is provided for attachment to the bicycle frame,
wherein the link base includes an attitude maintenance

mechanism for maintaining an attachment attitude of the
link base relative to the bicycle frame. The link base includes
a cylindrical component for fitting about the fixing pin, an
engagement component fixed to one end of the cylindrical
component, and an arm component fixed to another end of
the cylindrical component. The lower portion of the link
member is pivotally mounted to the arm component, and the

engagement component includes an engagement pin for
fitting in an opening formed on the bicycle frame. The shoe

attachment member and the brake arm are structured and

coupled so that one of the shoe attachment member or brake

arm sandwiches the other one of the shoe attachment mem
ber or brake arm.
