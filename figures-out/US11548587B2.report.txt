ABSTRACT
A vehicle includes a forward frame portion, a rear frame

portion, and a motion control system that movably inter
connects the forward frame portion and the rear frame
portion. A forward acceleration of the rear frame portion
resulting from a driving force imparted by a wheel supported
by the rear frame portion imparts a first force onto the
motion control system that counters a second force imparted
on the motion control system by an acceleration of a payload
supported by the forward frame portion as a result of the
forward acceleration .
