ABSTRACT
A pannier for a rear carrier rack on a bicycle carrier
rack on a bicycle comprises a bag of flexible, light
weight material and an internal frame having a back

part, a top part and a pair of upper side parts, each of
which is a network of interconnected ribs. The frame

imparts shape and stability to the bag, and the top part
has load-supporting capability so that the pannier top
serves as a lateral extension of the carrier rack.
