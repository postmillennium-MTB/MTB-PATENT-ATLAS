ABSTRACT

A sprocket shift assembly wherein sprockets of differ
ent diameters are arranged as a cluster on a common
shaft and are coupled with a chain between driving and
driven members. The chain is movable as the sprockets
rotate from one of the sprockets to another by realign
ment of portions of the sprocket such that the chain is at
all times in a positive driving relationship with the clus
ter.
