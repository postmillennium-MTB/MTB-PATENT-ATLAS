ABSTRACT

( 57 )

A derailleur system is provided that moves the derailleur
cage in a substantially rectilinear path . The derailleur is
mounted to a frame having a gear cassette mounted thereon .
The gear cassette includes an axis of rotation . The derailleur
includes a drive member engaging the gear cassette . The
derailleur is positioned on the frame adjacent the gear
cassette, and including a spatial linkage having a stationary
link , a floating link, and a cage assembly having two pulleys
each defining an axis of rotation . The drive member engages
each of the pulleys. The path of the floating link is substan
tially linear through substantially its entire range of motion
and variously aligns at least one of the pulleys with the gear
cassette .
