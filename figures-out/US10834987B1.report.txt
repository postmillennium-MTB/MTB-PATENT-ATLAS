ABSTRACT

Embodiments herein employ a novel strategy based on a
floating cellular liner that acts as a torsional suspension
system to dampen rotational acceleration, such as head
acceleration in a helmet, in response to an oblique impact.
Specifically, the torsional suspension consists of an aniso
tropic cellular liner that is at least partially recessed inside a
more rigid adjacent shell, relative to which the cellular liner
can simultaneously undergo translation and in - plane com

pression.
