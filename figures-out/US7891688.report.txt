A bicycle frame assembly includes an articulating linkage
mounting arrangement that fixedly couples one or more lever
arms to a rotatable axle. A preferred embodiment includes at
least one lever arm, and preferably a pair of lever arms. The
lever arm or arms are fixedly coupled to an axle by a fastener
having a fastener axis that is eccentric to, or offset from, the
axis of rotation of the axle. Accordingly, relative rotation of
the leverarm and axle is prevented such that the axle and lever
arm rotate together as a unit.
