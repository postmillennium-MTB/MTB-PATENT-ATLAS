ABSTRACT

( 57 )

A method and apparatus for a vehicle suspension system gas
spring . In one embodiment, a vehicle suspension system gas

spring includes a compressible main gas chamber and an

additional volume combinable with the main chamber to

change a gas spring rate of the system . In one embodiment,
a low friction piston seal is created by a flexible seal

member.
