ABSTRACT

The invention relates to the guiding of lines, or cables, on a
two-wheeled vehicle frame. The guiding is carried out with a
guide device on a frame element on which an additional frame
element is movably Supported in Such a way that the line
essentially intersects the rotational axis of the bearing.
