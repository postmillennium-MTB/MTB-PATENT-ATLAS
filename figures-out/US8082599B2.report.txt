ABSTRACT

A body protecting device for wearing by a user comprising an
array of energy absorbing cells, wherein each cell comprises
a tube, and wherein substantially each tube has a side wall
which is near or adjacent to the side wall of at least another
tube, and wherein Substantially each tube is configured Such
that the orientation of the tube is substantially maintained
when a load is applied parallel to the axis of the tube.
