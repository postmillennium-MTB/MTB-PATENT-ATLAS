ABSTRACT

A wheel hub for a bicycle wheel has a hub shell for receiving
spokes of the wheel and a sprocket body receives the
sprockets. The wheel hub and the sprocket body are con
nected through a free-wheel clutch. The roller bearings of
the sprocket body are freely axially displaceable on the axle.
An end member of the hub is screwed onto the sprocket-side
end of the axle and acts as a locking device for pressing the
sprocket body against the hub shell. To access the clutch and
for disassembling the hub, the end member can be screwed
off and the sprocket body can be pulled off the axle by hand.
This simplifies assembly, disassembly and maintenance of
the hub.
