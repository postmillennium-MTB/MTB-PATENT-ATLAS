ABSTRACT

The fixing device comprises a sole plate fixed to the sole
of a cyclist's shoe and comprising forward stop means
towards the front and a catch towards the rear. Re

straining means mounted on the pedal comprise for
ward stop means associated with those of the plate, and
a holding element movably mounted on the pedal and
capable of being applied by elastic biasing means against
the rear of said catch. The forward stop means and the
catch of the sole plate extend transversely under the
sole of the shoe, behind the sole portion having the
greatest width, while the associated forward stop means
mounted on the pedal extend transversely and are fixed
on the pedal behind the pedal spindle.
