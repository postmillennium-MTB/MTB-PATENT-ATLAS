ABSTRACT

A gear crank apparatus for a bicycle, comprising a
crank shaft, a pair of crank arms, and at least one chain
gear. The chain gear has an ellipse-like shape and pe
ripheral teeth located on different pitch diameters, the
chain gear being arranged with respect to the crank
arms and being coupled therewith, so that when torque
produced by a cyclist's leg during pedalling is maxi
mum, the gear diameter for transmitting the torque
from the chain gear to a driving chain becomes substan
tially minimum and when the torque is a minimum, the
gear diameter becomes substantially a maximum.
