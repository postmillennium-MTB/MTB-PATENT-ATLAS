ABSTRACT

Arear wheel suspension for a bicycle includes paired lower
arm members pivotally connected to the bicycle frame seat
tube such that they can pivot about an axis situated above the
center of the bottom bracket, paired upper arm members
adapted to receive the rear wheel axle at hub points located
on an axis situated above their lower ends and pivotally
connected to the respective lower arm members such that
they can pivot about an axis near the rear ends of the lower
arm members, and a shock absorber mechanism associated
with the upper ends of the upper arm members and pivotally
connected to an upper pivot point on the seat tube for
limiting the path and the extent of movement of the upper
ends of the upper arm members.
