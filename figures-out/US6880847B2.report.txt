ABSTRACT

(57)
A bicycle frame assembly having a main frame and an
articulating frame Supported for movement relative to the
main frame. The articulating frame is configured to carry a
rear wheel for rotation about a hub axis. A shock absorber is
operably connected between the main frame and the articu
lating frame to provide resistance to articulating motion of
the articulating frame. The articulating frame includes a
chain stay member and a shock Support member fixed for
angular displacement with the chain stay member.
