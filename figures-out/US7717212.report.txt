ABSTRACT

The invention relates to Suspension systems comprising, in
certain embodiments, a pivoting means concentric to a wheel
rotation axis so that braking forces can be controlled by
placement of an instant force center, and so that acceleration
forces can be controlled by a Swinging wheel link.
