ABSTRACT

A generally hollow bicycle frame of resin impregnated
fibrous material having a main tubular portion and a
cross tubular portion arranged in an X-shaped geometry
as viewed from one side. A rear segment of the main

portion may be slit to provide opposing rear stays for

receiving a rear wheel assembly therebetween. The
main tubular portion connects the head tube and the
rear wheel sugports and the cross tubular portion con

nects the bottom bracket and the seat sleeve. Both of

these portions may be substantially straight and continu
ous from end to end and joined by a generally hollow
common junction. Alternatively, the common junction
may integrally unite a plurality of pre-formed tubular
portions corresponding respectively to the supports for
the front wheel assembly, the rear wheel assembly, the
bottom bracket and the seat. The tubular portions may
have round or non-circular aerodynamic cross-sectional
shapes.
