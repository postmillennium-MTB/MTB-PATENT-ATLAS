ABSTRACT

A bicycle fork includes a pair of fork leg assemblies, each
of the leg assemblies having an upper leg telescopingly
engaged with a lower leg. A damping assembly is provided
in at least one of the legs. The damping assembly includes
an adjustable valve. Sometimes referred to as a pressure
relief or blow-off valve. The adjustable valve is externally
adjustable without tools. Furthermore, the adjustable valve
and another fluid flow control valve may be adjusted inde
pendently of each other.
