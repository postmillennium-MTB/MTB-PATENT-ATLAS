ABSTRACT

56

Novel handlebars for a bicycle are disclosed. The han
dlebars include a crosspiece connected to a bicycle
steering post, and first handles are connected to the
crosspiece. Novel second handles extend forwardly
from the crosspiece; these second handles are located so
that, when they are grasped by a rider, the rider's fore
arms are located to be supported by the handlebars at
positions over the crosspiece. The second handles are
located relatively close together to encourage the rider
