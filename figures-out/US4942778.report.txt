ABSTRACT

A clipless bicycle pedal system incorporating a pedal
and cleat, the pedal being a block with a generally con
vex symmetrical curved top and bottom surface and
being circular in the lateral plane. Grooves are formed
in the circumferential edge of the block, with at least
one groove each in the leading and trailing edges of the
block. The cleat is a plate which is to be fixed to the sole
of a rider's shoe and has a concave recess therein which
corresponds to the convex surface of the pedal block.
Within the recess is a spring-loaded tongue which is
adapted to be removably seated in the grooves of the
pedal block.
