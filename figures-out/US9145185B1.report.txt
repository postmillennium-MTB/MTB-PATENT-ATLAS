ABSTRACT

A bicycle frame includes front and rear parts, which are
pivotally connected to each other by upper and bottom links,
and a shock absorber. The bicycle frame is designed so that
compression in the shock absorber isolates the bicycle rider
from track noise. Additionally, energy loss caused by the
shock absorber compression during positive acceleration is
reduced.
