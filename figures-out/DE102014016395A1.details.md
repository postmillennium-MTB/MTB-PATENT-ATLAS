**Abstract:** A shiftable under load manual transmission, with a in a transmission housing (7) mounted input shaft (11) and output shaft (12), characterized by the following features: a) In the transmission housing (7) is at least one crown wheel (25), which at least one Crown sprocket (23) is constantly in meshing engagement. b) at least one crown wheel (25) and at least one crown wheel pinion (23) is indirectly connected to the output shaft (12) and input shaft (11) in connection. c) During one revolution of a crown wheel (25) the crown pinion (23) is not engaged with each tooth of the crown wheel (25). d) During more than one revolution of a crown wheel (25), the crown pinion (23) can engage with each tooth of the crown wheel (25). e) At least one crown wheel pinion (23) can be moved relative to the crown wheel (25) over a distance which is greater than twice the tooth width of the crown pinion (23).
