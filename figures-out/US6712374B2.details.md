**Abstract:** A two-wheeled vehicle, in particular a bicycle or a mountain bike, the vehicle being fitted with a rear suspension and comprising a front frame carrying a bottom bracket and an oscillating rear assembly carrying a driving rear wheel, said frame and said rear assembly being connected to each other via two pivot connections and via a damping system, wherein the first pivot connection is formed by a circular eccentric constituted in a casing carrying an eccentric pin, said casing being mounted to pivot in a housing in the front frame, while the rear assembly is fixed to the eccentric pin on either side of the casing, and wherein the second pivot connection is formed by a connecting rod constituted by a simple plate disposed in the general direction of the frame.
