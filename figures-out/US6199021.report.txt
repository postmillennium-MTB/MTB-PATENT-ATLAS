ABSTRACT

The invention broadly provides a method and apparatus for
measuring the power output of one powering a chain driven
vehicle. In a preferred embodiment described, the vehicle is
a bicycle. The method of the invention comprises measuring
the Speed and tension of the drive chain and calculating the
power output therefrom. The apparatus of the invention
includes a chain Speed Sensor, a chain tension Sensor, and
electronic processing means to calculate and display the
power output based upon the chain measurements.
